Questions

Frequently asked

Where are you based, and do you travel?
Alina is based in Voula, on the Athens Riviera. Sessions take place across Athens, the Greek islands and mainland Greece. Travel outside Athens, or outside Greece, is arranged separately as part of the booking.
How does pricing work?
Every session is quoted individually. Send an inquiry through the contact form describing what you have in mind, including the type of session, rough dates, location and how much coverage you need, and Alina will come back to you with a quote built around that.
Do you shoot film?
Yes. 35mm analog film is available as an add-on to any session or wedding collection, developed and scanned to high resolution. Each roll yields 10 to 15 scanned frames, delivered alongside the digital gallery.
How long until I get my photos?
Individual, couples and business sessions are delivered within two weeks. Weddings take longer, since a full day produces several hundred images and the timeline depends on the season and how many weddings are booked around yours. Your turnaround is agreed with you at booking, and a short preview from the day is sent within 72 hours.
What languages do you speak?
English, Ukrainian and Russian, plus conversational Greek.

Not sure what you need? I am open to creative projects, collaborations and ideas that do not fit neatly into a category. Tell me what you have in mind.
